### Organic Traffic Growth Plan

1. **Optimize Product Listings**: Ensure that all product descriptions are rich in keywords related to handmade cards, occasions, and unique features. Use tools like Google Keyword Planner to find relevant keywords that potential customers are searching for.

2. **Content Marketing**: Start a blog on your shop’s website. Write articles about topics like "How to Choose the Perfect Greeting Card" or "Unique Uses for Handmade Cards." This will not only attract organic traffic but also establish you as an expert in your niche.

3. **Collect and Display Reviews**: Encourage customers to leave reviews. Positive feedback can improve your shop's credibility and search visibility. Consider offering a discount on future purchases in exchange for a review.

4. **Leverage Social Media**: Regularly share content related to your products on platforms like Instagram and Pinterest. Use trending hashtags and engage with your audience to drive traffic back to your shop.

5. **Participate in Craft Fairs or Local Markets**: These events can help you gain local visibility and direct traffic back to your Etsy shop. Promote these events on your social media accounts and encourage attendees to check out your online offerings.

### Pinterest Strategy

1. **Posting Frequency**: Aim to post 3-5 pins daily. Consistency is key on Pinterest; the more you pin, the more likely you are to gain visibility.

2. **Board Structure**: Create boards based on occasions (e.g., "Mother’s Day Cards," "Funny Greeting Cards," "Seasonal Cards"). Make sure to include a mix of your products and related content (like card-making tips or DIY projects).

3. **Pin Types**: Use a mix of product pins (highlighting your cards), infographics (like card-giving etiquette), and lifestyle images (showing your cards in use). Video pins can also be effective for showing the card-making process or unboxing experiences.

4. **Best Days to Post**: Pin mainly on weekends (especially Saturday), as users tend to browse Pinterest for ideas and inspiration during their free time. Monitor your analytics to find the best times for your audience.

5. **Engagement**: Engage with your audience by repinning relevant content from other creators and responding to comments on your pins. This helps build community and increases your visibility.
